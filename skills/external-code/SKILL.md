# External code

Inspect the exact source revision and its license before building on it.

1. **Permissive license** (for example MIT, Apache-2.0, BSD, or ISC): copy or
   adapt within its terms and retain required copyright, license, attribution,
   and notice material.
2. **Copyleft or source-available license**: identify the obligations and stop
   for an explicit compatibility decision before copying, adapting, linking,
   or distributing into a differently licensed project.
3. **No license stated**: treat the source as copyrighted with no permission to
   copy, adapt, derive from, vendor, or redistribute its expression. Public
   visibility is not a license. Use only uncopyrightable facts and ideas that
   can be independently re-expressed, and preserve the source identity in the
   research record.
4. **All-rights-reserved, non-commercial, or no-derivatives terms**: stop before
   using the source as implementation material unless the terms or separate
   permission clearly authorize the intended use.

Distinguish studying an idea from copying expression. Record the source URL,
exact revision or release, license identity, copied or adapted scope, and
retained notices. If that boundary is unresolved, do not derive the candidate
implementation.
